## Why

After at least one year of stable traffic and proven marketplace operations, synchronous learning, community participation, and commercial partnerships can deepen retention and diversify revenue. These higher-risk capabilities must remain gated until moderation, safety, finance, and operations can support them.

## What Changes

- Add live courses with interactive audio/video and long-form course series.
- Add online cooking competitions and offline cooking-salon registration.
- Add student forums, recipe-photo sharing, and tutor-fan communities with moderation and reporting.
- Add workflows for brand partnerships, sponsored content, cross-border kitchenware/sauce promotion, and culinary study-tour leads.
- Preserve offshore-only distribution, original-content rules, sponsorship disclosure, and prohibited domestic promotion channels.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `live-learning`: Scheduled interactive broadcasts, attendance, recordings, safety controls, and long-form series.
- `events-and-competitions`: Online competition entries/judging and offline salon registration and consent.
- `cooking-community`: Forums, photo posts, tutor communities, reporting, moderation, and privacy controls.
- `commercial-partnerships`: Brand/sponsorship campaigns, disclosures, product promotion, and study-tour lead governance.

### Modified Capabilities

None. This change depends on, but does not redefine, Phase 1 and Phase 2 capabilities; its new capabilities specify their integration constraints explicitly.

## Impact

Adds real-time media infrastructure, event workflows, community storage and moderation, notification and safety services, sponsorship inventory, and lead-management integrations to the C# backend and Flutter clients. This change depends on successful Phase 1 and Phase 2 operation and explicit launch approval.
