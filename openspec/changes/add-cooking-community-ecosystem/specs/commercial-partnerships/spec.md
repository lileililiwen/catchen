## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Brand and sponsorship campaigns
Authorized staff SHALL manage approved offshore kitchenware and sauce brand campaigns with contracts, targeting, tutor eligibility, deliverables, compensation, dates, asset approvals, and mandatory sponsorship disclosures.

#### Scenario: Sponsored content displayed
- **WHEN** a user views sponsored content
- **THEN** the client displays a clear localized advertising or sponsorship disclosure adjacent to the content

### Requirement: Tutor partnership opportunities
Eligible tutors SHALL view, apply for, accept, deliver, and report on brand opportunities without exposing another tutor's confidential terms.

#### Scenario: Sponsored deliverable approved
- **WHEN** staff approves a contracted tutor deliverable
- **THEN** any owed compensation enters an auditable contract-specific settlement workflow

### Requirement: Culinary study-tour leads
The system SHALL present vetted overseas culinary study-tour offers and collect only consented lead data for a named provider, purpose, retention period, and destination jurisdiction.

#### Scenario: Lead consent withheld
- **WHEN** a user does not provide specific transfer consent
- **THEN** no personal data is sent to the tour provider

### Requirement: Offshore channel enforcement
Partnerships and tours MUST NOT target Mainland China or use Xiaohongshu, Douyin, domestic WeChat groups, domestic APK distribution, or domestic payment methods.

#### Scenario: Campaign includes prohibited channel
- **WHEN** a partnership campaign contains a prohibited domestic channel
- **THEN** the system blocks campaign approval and records the failed compliance check
