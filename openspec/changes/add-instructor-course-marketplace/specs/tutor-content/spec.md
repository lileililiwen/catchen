## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Tutor recipe and course publishing
Approved tutors SHALL submit free text/image recipes and paid segmented-video courses; every item MUST contain original assets, rights evidence, standardized quantities, substitutions, Western-kitchenware guidance, cultural context, and English consumer content.

#### Scenario: Course submitted for review
- **WHEN** a tutor submits a complete paid course
- **THEN** it remains unavailable to consumers until content, copyright, substitution, and usability reviews pass

### Requirement: Paid segmented-video access
The client SHALL present ordered short-video segments, progress, captions or transcripts, and supporting recipe materials only to entitled purchasers, using expiring media authorization.

#### Scenario: Unentitled video request
- **WHEN** a user without a valid course entitlement requests a protected segment
- **THEN** the system denies media authorization and exposes only the course preview

### Requirement: Tutor content operations
Administrators SHALL review, request revision, approve, publish, unpublish, and preserve versions and evidence for tutor recipes and courses.

#### Scenario: Copyright complaint
- **WHEN** a credible rights complaint is recorded
- **THEN** affected content can be unpublished immediately while evidence and purchaser records remain auditable
